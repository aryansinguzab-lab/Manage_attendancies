🛡️ The Privacy Story (How it works)

When you click "Register," this app doesn't save your photo. Instead, the AI looks at the geometry of your face,the distance between eyes, the curve of your jaw and converts it into a 128-bit mathematical vector. It will relate the next capture to the stored geometric features and records the individual entrance/exit.

Think of it like a digital fingerprint made of numbers. Even if someone stole the database, they couldn't see your face; they would only see a list of random numbers.

1. DATA LOCALITY

All biometric descriptors and attendance logs are stored strictly in the browser's IndexedDB on this specific device. No data is sent to the cloud, Google, or any external server.

2. USER CONSENT

The Administrator (main user) of this device is responsible for obtaining explicit consent from all individuals before registering their facial data. Use of this system for unauthorized surveillance is strictly prohibited.

3. ACCURACY & LIMITATIONS

While the ACSOL AI is highly advanced, factors such as extreme lighting, face masks, or identical twins may affect recognition accuracy. The system is provided "as is" without a 100% accuracy guarantee. It uses 10 minutes interval to record the same person consecutively to avoid unnecessary repeated records and overcrowding the database
